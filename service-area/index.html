Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if I'm inside your service area?

We cover Oxford and Lafayette County within about 30 miles. If you're close to that edge or not sure, just call — we'll tell you honestly whether we can get to you.

Do you serve Water Valley, Taylor, or Abbeville?

Those towns sit near the edge of our typical coverage area. Give us a call with your location and we'll let you know if we can reach you.

Do you cover Sardis Lake?

The Sardis Lake area generally falls within our service radius. Call and give us your specific location and we'll confirm.

Is Ole Miss campus part of your service area?

Yes — campus, dorm lots, and student housing around Oxford are some of our most common calls. See our Ole Miss roadside assistance page for details.
