Frequently Asked Questions

What are your hours?

We're on call Monday through Saturday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and closed on Sundays. If you break down outside those hours, leave your name and number on our contact page and you'll be the first call we make when we're back open.

How much does a jump start or gas delivery cost?

Pricing depends on your location and the service you need. Visit our Pricing & How It Works page for typical costs and exactly what to expect before we arrive — no surprise invoices.

Do you come out to Ole Miss campus and student housing?

Yes. We regularly help students in campus lots, dorm parking, and off-campus housing around Oxford. See our Ole Miss roadside assistance page for details on parking access and game-day considerations.

What areas around Oxford do you serve?

We cover Oxford and Lafayette County within about 30 miles, including the Highway 6 and Highway 7 corridors. Check our Service Area page for the full rundown.

I ran out of gas on the highway — what do I do?

Get your hazards on, pull as far onto the shoulder as safely possible, and stay buckled in your car with your seatbelt on until help arrives if you're near traffic. Then call us and tell us your mile marker or the nearest exit — we'll bring enough fuel to get you to the nearest station.
