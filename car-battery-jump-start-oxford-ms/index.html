Signs it's your battery

  • Nothing happens when you turn the key — no lights, no sound
  • You hear a single click, then silence
  • The engine cranks slowly, like it's straining
  • Dashboard lights are dim or flicker when you try to start

If any of that sounds familiar, searching "jump start near me" and calling us is your fastest path back on the road.

What we do when we arrive

  1. Check the battery terminals and connections
  2. Jump start your car with our equipment
  3. Let the engine run a few minutes to confirm it holds a charge
  4. Give you an honest read on whether the battery itself needs replacing
Why This Happens So Much Here

Mississippi weather is hard on batteries

Summer heat wears down battery internals faster than most people expect, and a cold snap in January can be enough to finally finish off a battery that was already on its way out. If you're dealing with a dead battery in Oxford more than once, it's usually a sign the battery itself is near the end of its life, not bad luck. Frequently Asked Questions

How can I tell if it's really the battery?

If you turn the key and get nothing — no lights on the dash, no clicking, no crank at all — that's almost always a dead battery. A single click or a slow, straining crank usually points the same direction. If your lights and radio work fine but the engine just won't turn over, it could be something else, but a jump start is still the fastest first thing to try.

How much does a jump start cost in Oxford?

It depends on your location and time of day. Visit our Pricing & How It Works page for typical costs, or just call and we'll give you a straight answer before we head out.

Will you tell me if I need a new battery instead?

Yes. If your battery is old or won't hold a charge after we jump it, we'll tell you honestly so you're not calling us again next week. We don't sell batteries, but we'll let you know what we're seeing.
