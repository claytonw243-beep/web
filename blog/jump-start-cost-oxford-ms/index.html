How Much Does a Jump Start Cost? (Oxford, MS Pricing Guide)

If you’ve never called for a roadside jump start before, not knowing the price ahead of time is almost as stressful as the dead battery itself. Here’s what actually goes into the cost, so you know what questions to ask.

What affects the price

Roadside jump start pricing generally comes down to a handful of factors:

  • Where you are. A jump start in the middle of Oxford costs less to get to than one 20 miles out toward Water Valley or Taylor.
  • What time it is. Some companies charge more outside normal business hours — we don’t operate outside our posted hours at all, so this isn’t something you’ll run into with us.
  • What’s actually wrong. A straightforward jump start is one price; if it turns out you also need something else looked at, that changes things.
  • How the company prices its base call-out fee. Some charge a flat rate, others price by distance or by the hour.

What you should ask before agreeing to anything

Any roadside company worth calling should be able to answer these on the phone, before they ever leave to come find you:

  1. What’s the total cost for a jump start at my location?
  2. Does that change if it turns out to be something other than the battery?
  3. Do you charge extra for distance or time of day?
  4. What payment methods do you take?

If a company won’t give you a straight answer to those questions, that’s worth noticing.

Our approach

We give you a clear price on the phone before we start driving — not an estimate that turns into something bigger once we show up. See our Pricing & How It Works page for the specifics, or just call and ask directly.

Is a jump start ever not worth it?

If your battery needs jumping every few weeks, you’re probably spending more over time calling for repeat jump starts than you would just replacing the battery. We’ll tell you honestly if that’s what we’re seeing when we come out.
