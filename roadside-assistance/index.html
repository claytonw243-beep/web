Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if I need a jump start or fuel delivery?

If your car won't start at all — no lights, no clicking, nothing — that's almost always a dead battery. If the engine cranks like normal but won't catch and your gauge reads empty, that's fuel. Not sure? Just call and describe what's happening and we'll figure it out together.

Do you offer towing?

We specialize in jump starts and fuel delivery, not towing. If your issue turns out to be something we can't fix roadside, we'll tell you honestly and point you toward a tow company.

How fast can you get to me?

It depends on where you are and how busy we are at the moment, but we'll give you an honest estimate on the phone before you commit to anything. See our Pricing & How It Works page for more detail.

What areas do you cover?

We serve Oxford and Lafayette County within about 30 miles, including the Ole Miss campus and the Highway 6 and Highway 7 corridors.
