Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a jump start or gas delivery actually cost?

It depends on your location and the specific service. Call us and we'll give you a clear price before we head your way — you'll never be surprised by the bill once we arrive.

Do you charge extra for distance?

Pricing can vary with distance from Oxford. If you're near the edge of our 30-mile service area, ask about that when you call.

Do I pay before or after you help me?

You'll know the price before we start any work — nothing happens without you agreeing to it first.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash, Credit Card, Apple Pay, Venmo.
