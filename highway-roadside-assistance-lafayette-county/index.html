If you break down on the shoulder

  1. Turn your hazard lights on immediately
  2. Signal and move as far onto the shoulder as you safely can
  3. Stay buckled in your seat if you're close to moving traffic, rather than standing outside
  4. Call us with your direction of travel, the nearest exit or mile marker, and any landmark you can see

For more detail, read our blog post onroadside safety on a Mississippi highway.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I don't know my exact mile marker?

That's fine — tell us your direction of travel, the last exit or town you passed, and any landmark you can see (a business sign, a bridge, a mile marker if you spot one). We can work with that.

Is it safer to stay in my car or get out?

If you're stopped close to moving traffic, staying buckled in your seat is generally safer than standing outside the car. If you can safely exit away from the traffic side, do that instead. Always keep your hazard lights on.

Do you cover the whole stretch toward Batesville and I-55?

We cover Highway 6 within our normal service radius. If you're farther out toward I-55, call and give us your location — we'll tell you honestly if it's within reach.

What about Highway 7 toward Water Valley and Pontotoc?

Same answer — we cover Highway 7 within our typical range around Oxford. Give us a call with your location and we'll confirm.
