Why we do this

Oxford is a small town with a big weekend population — students, game-day visitors, and everyone driving Highway 6 and Highway 7 in between. When your car lets you down here, you deserve someone who'll show up, tell you honestly what's wrong, and charge you a fair price for fixing it — not a stranger reading a script off a national dispatch board.

That's the whole idea behind Breakdown Buddies: be the neighbor you'd actually want to call.
